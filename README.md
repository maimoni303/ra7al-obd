# RA7AL OBD

Free iPhone + CarPlay app for the ZUS/NONDA "OBDBLE" Bluetooth adapter, tuned for a 2019 Ford F-250 6.7 diesel.

- Live gauges with dashboard symbols (coolant, oil, diesel range, battery, rail pressure, tyres)
- Warnings by notification, Arabic voice and CarPlay alert (coolant/oil temperature, low diesel & range, low tyres, charging, MIL)
- Diesel range learned from real consumption; refuel detection
- Oil life from distance, engine hours and idle hours
- Automatic full scan when the truck starts (stored, pending and permanent DTCs, VIN with check-digit, tyres)
- Nearest diesel station with Google Maps hand-off (also on CarPlay)
- AZDOME PG17 dash-cam: live view and clips over its Wi-Fi (phone only)
- Three themes: night, day, youth
- Shortcut «فحص شامل» for a "When CarPlay connects" automation

Adapter protocol notes: `docs/PROTOCOL-NOTES.md`. The original probe lives on as Settings ▸ أداة فحص القطعة.
